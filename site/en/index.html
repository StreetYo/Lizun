Everything you need, in one island

The island stays out of your way: it doesn't take the keyboard from your apps, and clicks next to it go to the windows below.

Music from any app

Spotify, music in the browser, Telegram: whatever plays on your PC shows up in the island with its cover and title, plus pause and next or previous track.

A tray for your files

Drop files onto the island and they wait there. Later, drag them out into any folder, email or chat.

Everything you copied, at hand

Text, pictures and files you copy are kept in the Clipboard tab. Click one to copy it again. Passwords that apps mark as secret are never saved.

Tasks right in the island

A short to-do list always at the top of the screen. The closed island shows your next task.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Lizun is free and open source: all the code is on GitHub for anyone to check.

Why does Windows show a warning?

The app doesn't have a paid code signature yet, so SmartScreen doesn't know it and warns just in case. Click “More info”, then “Run anyway”.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit). A macOS version is planned. Lizun doesn't run on phones: it's an app for your computer.

Will it get in the way?

No. The island opens when you point at it and closes when the cursor leaves. It doesn't take the keyboard from your apps, and clicks next to it go to the windows below. You can also hide it, in Settings or from the tray icon.

How do I uninstall it?

Like any other app: Settings → Apps → Lizun → Uninstall.

Is it finished?

It's an early version: the main things work, but expect some rough edges. Found a bug or have an idea? Tell us on GitHub.
